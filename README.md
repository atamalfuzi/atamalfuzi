# Hi, I'm Ata Malfuzi

Data scientist and economist in Vancouver, BC. I build data pipelines, econometric models and energy-economic systems that turn complex datasets into decisions for government, research and policy.

- **Energy-economic modelling:** transportation electrification, carbon markets, policy scenario analysis
- **Applied econometrics:** time series, state-space models, panel data, index construction
- **Stack:** R, Python, SQL, Julia, Azure, Git

## Work samples

Personal projects built from publicly available data. They are not associated with my work for any current or past employer and do not represent their views.

- [bc_electricity_balance](https://github.com/atamalfuzi/bc_electricity_balance): why BC imported a sixth of its electricity in the 2023 drought, what the trade cost, and how demand and its response to temperature have changed since 1980, from open data with a [monthly dashboard](https://atamalfuzi.github.io/bc_electricity_balance/) (Python, statsmodels)
- [canada_gas_demand](https://github.com/atamalfuzi/canada_gas_demand): how households in BC, Ontario and Alberta respond to natural gas prices since 1981, in the short and long run, with a Kalman-filter time-varying elasticity and two estimation traps (Python, statsmodels)
- [bc_pop_change_map](https://github.com/atamalfuzi/bc_pop_change_map): maps of population change across British Columbia's census subdivisions (R, sf, ggplot2)
- [bc_housing_affordability](https://github.com/atamalfuzi/bc_housing_affordability): rent and mortgage costs as a share of family income across BC's cities, from open data (R, ggplot2)
